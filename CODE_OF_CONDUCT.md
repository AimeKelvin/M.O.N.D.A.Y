# Community code of conduct

MONDAY welcomes contributors of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

Be respectful, discuss technical ideas in good faith, give actionable feedback, and credit others' work. Respect privacy and rights to shared models. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, unwanted attention, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive conduct are unacceptable in project spaces and when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban repeat or serious offenders. Decisions should be proportionate and protect affected people. Participants should comply with reasonable moderation requests.

Report concerns privately to a listed repository maintainer through the host's private contact/reporting tools. Do not post another person's personal information or confidential evidence in a public issue. No dedicated reporting email is configured in this initial repository; the project owner should publish an appropriate private contact before broad community launch.
